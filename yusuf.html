Stories of the Prophets

Prophet Yusuf (AS)

Prophet Yusuf (AS) was one of the beloved prophets of Allah. His brothers became jealous of him and threw him into a well. Later, he was taken to Egypt and sold as a servant.

Although he faced many hardships, Yusuf (AS) remained patient and trusted Allah. He was later imprisoned unjustly, but Allah raised him to a position of honor where he became a trusted leader.

When he finally met his brothers again, he forgave them instead of taking revenge. His story teaches us patience, forgiveness, sincerity, and complete trust in Allah.

Lessons

  • Always trust Allah.
  • Be patient during difficulties.
  • Forgive those who wrong you.
  • Good character brings success.
