Remember Allah throughout your day with authentic supplications.

🌙 Everyday Duas

Simple supplications to learn and practice.

🌅 Morning Dua

اللَّهُمَّ بِكَ أَصْبَحْنَا، وَبِكَ أَمْسَيْنَا، وَبِكَ نَحْيَا، وَبِكَ نَمُوتُ، وَإِلَيْكَ النُّشُورُ

"O Allah, by You we enter the morning, by You we enter the evening..."

🍽️ Before Eating

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ

"In the name of Allah."

🍽️ After Eating

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ

"All praise is for Allah."

🏠 Leaving Home

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ، تَوَكَّلْتُ عَلَى اللَّهِ، وَلَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ

"In the name of Allah, I place my trust in Allah. There is no power or strength except with Allah."

🛏️ Before Sleeping

بِاسْمِكَ اللَّهُمَّ أَمُوتُ وَأَحْيَا

"In Your name, O Allah, I die and I live."

🤲 Dua for Knowledge

رَبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا

"My Lord, increase me in knowledge."

✨ A Daily Reminder

Make remembrance of Allah part of your everyday life. Even a small, sincere act of remembrance can be meaningful.

Remember Allah Wherever You Are.
