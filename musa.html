Prophet Musa (AS)

Prophet Musa (AS) was chosen by Allah to guide the Children of Israel. As a baby, Allah protected him by inspiring his mother to place him in a basket on the river.

When he grew older, Allah sent him to call Pharaoh to worship Allah alone. Despite Pharaoh's arrogance, Musa (AS) remained courageous and relied completely on Allah.

Allah performed many miracles through Musa (AS), including parting the sea to save the believers. His story teaches courage, faith, patience, and trust in Allah.

  • Trust Allah in every situation.
  • Stand up for what is right.
  • Be patient during trials.
  • Allah always helps the believers.
